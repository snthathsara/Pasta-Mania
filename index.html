🍝 Authentic Italian Casual Dining

Every plate begins with bronze-die al dente pasta, rich Italian tomatoes, and genuine culinary passion.

Located at 502 R A De Mel Mawatha and Level 05, Food Studio at One Galle Face Mall, PastaMania is Colombo's premier Italian casual dining destination. We cook your pasta fresh to order with authentic Italian ingredients — from rich slow-simmered minced beef Bolognese and silky egg-yolk Carbonara to fragrant olive oil Prawn Aglio Olio. Choose your preferred pasta shape (Spaghetti, Penne, Fusilli or Linguine), indulge in golden garlic bread with bubbling melted mozzarella, and complete your feast with handcrafted Italian espresso and refreshing spritzers.
